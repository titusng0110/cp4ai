# Copy 4 AI 3.1.0

Copy 4 AI turns Word, PDF and PowerPoint files into clean text that you can
paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any other AI chat.

## What's new

- **A proper Mac app.** Copy 4 AI has been rebuilt from the ground up. It is
  faster, lighter, and there is nothing else to install.
- **Two Finder Services.** Choose **Copy 4 AI** to read text from images
  too, or **Copy 4 AI (no OCR)** for a faster run that only reads text already
  in the file.
- **Command-line tools included.** The first time you open the app, it also
  installs `cp4ai` and `md4ai` for anyone who likes using the terminal.
- **A short reminder.** Double-click the app at any time to see what was
  installed and how to use it.
- **Everything stays on your Mac.** Files are converted locally. Nothing is
  uploaded, and no account is needed.

## Install

1. Download `Copy4AI-3.1.0-arm64.dmg`.
2. Open the DMG and drag **Copy 4 AI.app** to Applications.
3. Open **Copy 4 AI** once and click **OK**.
4. In Finder, select your files, right-click → **Services**, and choose
   **Copy 4 AI** or **Copy 4 AI (no OCR)**.

If macOS says it cannot verify the app, right-click the app and choose
**Open** instead of double-clicking.

## Good to know

- Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 15 or later.
- The first launch may take a few seconds while macOS registers the Services.
- **Copy 4 AI** reads text from pictures and scans. **Copy 4 AI (no OCR)** is
  faster and skips pictures; use it when your files already contain
  selectable text.
- If you used version 3.0, simply replace the old app with this one.
- To use the terminal tools, open a new Terminal window and type
  `cp4ai --help` or `md4ai --help`.
